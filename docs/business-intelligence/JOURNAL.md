# Founder Journal

Status: working notes. This is the landing zone for new business insight from working sessions, before it's mature enough to become a permanent, numbered `PRINCIPLES.md` entry. Entries here are dated and can be exploratory; `PRINCIPLES.md` entries are not.

## How to use this file

1. When a new business rule, philosophy point, or operational insight comes up in a session, add a dated entry here first.
2. When it's clearly a durable, general principle (not a one-off decision), promote it to `PRINCIPLES.md` as the next numbered entry, and note here which principle number it became.
3. Never let a real decision sit only in chat — if it isn't here or in a permanent doc by the end of the session, it hasn't been captured (see `CLAUDE.md`'s Knowledge Capture Policy).

### 2026-08-02 — Knowledge Capture Session 002: DEFERRD and Operations Compliance Engine approved

Founder approved two new HURKL/Mason product capabilities, plus four new core principles, all recorded permanently rather than left in chat:

- **DEFERRD** approved as HURKL's first-party physical communication and fulfillment service — the customer chooses an outcome (e.g. "send the new-homeowner campaign"), never a mailing vendor; Mason handles trigger detection, eligibility, template selection, personalization, fulfillment, status/cost tracking, and audit logging behind the scenes. Full detail in `DEFERRD_FULFILLMENT.md`.
- **Event-driven physical communication** — mail is triggered by meaningful business events (property sale detected, job completed, birthdays/anniversaries, referrals, certifications, safety milestones, stale estimates, inactive customers, seasonal events), not by the owner remembering to run campaigns. Every automatic action stays subject to an owner-approved playbook, spending limits, lawful data use, audit logging, deduplication, and cancellation.
- **Operations Compliance Engine** approved — tracks employee, company, equipment/vehicle, and project-specific qualifications (licenses, insurance, bonding, certifications, inspections, registrations, and more) across eight statuses, with configurable lead-time warnings (180 down to 7 days). Connects compliance status directly to business consequences (e.g., a specific bid opportunity at risk from an expiring certification) rather than sending generic reminders. Mason must never fabricate that a credential is valid — unverified records are labeled as such. Full detail in `OPERATIONS_COMPLIANCE.md`.
- **Expiration prevention** — Principle 007: a reminder that arrives after expiration is a system failure; the intended behavior is early detection, escalation, and resolution planning, well before a deadline causes real harm (lost bid eligibility, jobsite removal, work stoppage, etc.).
- **Compliance linked to bid/project eligibility** — before recommending an opportunity, Mason now evaluates compliance status (expiring credentials during the project period, workforce/equipment/bonding capacity) alongside the existing qualification factors, classifying any gap as impossible-before-deadline, potentially-correctable, or a long-term growth-path item — see `QUALIFICATION_ENGINE.md`'s new "Connection to the Operations Compliance Engine and Opportunity Engine" section.
- **Mason removing mental load** (Principle 004) and **intelligent delegation** (Principle 005) — owners want fewer things to remember and manage, not more software to operate; Mason automates routine pre-approved decisions and only interrupts the owner for genuine strategic decisions, exceptions, risk, budget thresholds, or legal commitments.

Promoted to `PRINCIPLES.md` as **004** (Remove Mental Load), **005** (Intelligent Delegation), **006** (Hide Operational Complexity), and **007** (Prevent Problems Before They Exist).

Two dependency-free TypeScript domain-contract files added ahead of implementation (types only, no runtime logic, no database, no real provider): `lib/domain/fulfillment.ts` and `lib/domain/compliance.ts`. No proprietary scoring/research logic, no customer-visible alternate mailing providers, and no third-party dependencies were introduced.

### 2026-08-02 — Knowledge Capture Session 003: core philosophy strengthened; Verified Intelligence, Evidence-Based Operations, and Continuous Business Intelligence approved

Founder approved a large set of philosophy strengthening and three new principles, plus two new growth-adjacent playbooks under DEFERRD, all recorded permanently rather than left in chat:

- **Core philosophy strengthened** — `OWNER_PHILOSOPHY.md` gained a new **Owner Vision** bullet (success is whatever the owner defines it as — a small profitable team, more family time, a commercial operation, a regional company — Mason's job is to help build toward whichever one the owner picks, not to assume bigger is better), richer wording under Owner Sovereignty and Remove Mental Load, and a new **Progressive Delegation** subsection: Mason earns autonomy over time rather than assuming it, expanding only within explicit boundaries (scope, budget, frequency, recipient eligibility, legal/compliance rules, cancellation rights, audit records) as the owner approves recurring playbooks — and still always interrupts the owner for strategic decisions, unusual exceptions, meaningful risk, legal/contractual commitments, over-budget spending, or uncertain/conflicting information. `CAPACITY_ENGINE.md` gained a matching **Owner Vision** section with the same four example destinations. Principles 001, 004, and 005 already covered this territory, so this round elaborated the practical docs rather than editing the finalized numbered entries (append-only rule).
- **Verified Intelligence** approved — Mason must never fabricate knowledge, confidence, completion, compliance, eligibility, or source authority; when it doesn't know something, it says so and researches the verified answer rather than guessing, using a six-tier source hierarchy (verified company records → official government/regulatory sources → official manufacturer/software documentation → official industry associations/standards bodies → other reliable sources → professional review required) and always distinguishes a direct verified statement from a plain-language explanation, an inference, a recommendation, or an unresolved ambiguity. No live web research crawler exists yet — this is a principle Mason's future research capability must be built to satisfy, not something implemented today. Full detail in new file `VERIFIED_INTELLIGENCE.md`.
- **Evidence-Based Operations** approved — every important recommendation preserves a durable evidence record (source, jurisdiction, summarized requirement, affected subject, confidence level, interpretation uncertainty, what was recommended vs. done, who approved it, and a re-verification date), not just a one-time answer. Full detail in new file `EVIDENCE_BASED_OPERATIONS.md`. New dependency-free domain-contract file `lib/domain/evidence.ts` added with the nine founder-specified types: `EvidenceSource`, `EvidenceRecord`, `RegulatoryRequirement`, `VerifiedFinding`, `RecommendationEvidence`, `Jurisdiction`, `VerificationStatus`, `SourceAuthorityLevel`, `ReverificationRule` — types only, no runtime logic, no crawler, no database schema.
- **Continuous Business Intelligence** approved — "Our principles are permanent. Our knowledge is always evolving." Trades and regulations change continuously (laws, codes, OSHA rules, standards, products, equipment, certifications, bid systems, software, suppliers, market conditions, customer expectations); new information must never automatically become company policy without a detect → verify → preserve-evidence → determine-affected-subjects → recommend → request-approval → update-guidance → schedule-re-verification cycle. Explicitly a future capability — no live web research crawler exists yet. Full detail in new file `CONTINUOUS_INTELLIGENCE.md`.
- **Direct Mail — Fallback Lead Generation** approved as a DEFERRD-tied playbook — triggered when other lead sources are insufficient for a company's capacity goals; the founder's anecdotal baseline (approximately one meaningful job per roughly 500 households reached) is recorded explicitly labeled as an anecdotal founder observation, not a guaranteed conversion rate, consistent with the new Verified Intelligence principle; tracked metrics (households reached, cost, responses, jobs won, cost-per-job) let Mason report real outcomes rather than assume the baseline holds. Recorded in `DEFERRD_FULFILLMENT.md`.
- **Persistent Physical Advertising** approved — founder-approved principle that certain trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, carpet cleaning, landscaping, pest control, appliance repair, garage door repair, locksmith, pool service, restoration, handyman, chimney service, pressure washing, window cleaning) benefit from durable physical branding (e.g. magnets) left behind after a job, reasoned as low-cost persistent visibility distinct from one-time mail; future DEFERRD workflows will handle ordering/tracking. Recorded in `DEFERRD_FULFILLMENT.md`, which also gained explicit **Customer** and **Employee** fulfillment-outcome categories.
