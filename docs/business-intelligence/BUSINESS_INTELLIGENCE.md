# Business Intelligence — Mason's Permanent Knowledge Base

Status: permanent, living documentation, established 2026-08-02 per the founder's Knowledge Capture Policy (see `CLAUDE.md`).

**This is not application code.** It is the knowledge base Mason will eventually draw on to make business-growth judgment calls on the owner's behalf, and the permanent record of the business philosophy and principles the founder has established for how Mason operates as a growth partner — not just an office manager.

## Founding Insight

> We are not building software that simply automates tasks. We are building software that helps business owners build the company they envision. Mason succeeds when the owner's vision succeeds.

This is the idea the rest of this knowledge base exists to serve — recorded in Knowledge Capture Session 001 (2026-08-02, see `JOURNAL.md`). It's the deeper articulation of `PRODUCT.md`'s mission ("Helping Business Owners Reclaim Their Lives") and Product Principle ("We help companies grow to the size of their vision") — the same spirit, stated as plainly as possible.

## What this is, and how it differs from the rest of the docs

- `PRODUCT.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `SECURITY.md` define the **platform**: what Mason is, how it's built, and what it's allowed to do.
- This section defines the **business judgment** Mason applies once the growth/opportunity capabilities in `PRODUCT.md`'s "Growth & capacity management" section are actually built (see `ROADMAP.md`'s "Future phase — Growth & capacity management," not yet sequenced).
- It is a living journal plus a growing, numbered set of permanent principles — so decisions made in conversation with the founder become durable, structured knowledge instead of one-off chat history.

## Privacy boundary — same rule as the rest of this public repository

**`hurkl-platform` is currently public.** Everything in this Business Intelligence section states principles, philosophy, and categories only. It intentionally does **not** contain: opportunity-scoring formulas, detailed commercial research methods, outreach tactics, data-source discovery methods, signal timing/staging rules, territory-matching logic, or the founder's private business-development playbooks — see `PRODUCT.md`'s "Proprietary methods" section, which this knowledge base is bound by exactly the same way. That detailed, proprietary logic will eventually live in protected, non-public configuration and internal rules (see `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s "Future services" note) — not in this repository, and not in these files, unless the founder explicitly authorizes disclosure.
