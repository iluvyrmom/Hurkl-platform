# Continuous Intelligence Engine

Status: permanent knowledge base entry, foundational. Elaborates Principle 010, `PRINCIPLES.md`. **Future capability, not yet built** — no live web research crawler exists yet.

## Principle

**"Our principles are permanent. Our knowledge is always evolving."**

Trades and businesses change continuously, through: new laws, revised building codes, OSHA changes, new safety standards, new products and materials, new equipment, changing certification requirements, changing bid systems, new software, changing supplier relationships, changing market conditions, and changing customer expectations.

**New information must never automatically become company policy without verification and impact review.** This is the same discipline as `VERIFIED_INTELLIGENCE.md`, applied over time rather than at a single point.

## The eight-step process

A future Continuous Intelligence Engine should eventually:

1. Detect meaningful changes.
2. Verify them through authoritative sources (see `VERIFIED_INTELLIGENCE.md`'s source hierarchy).
3. Preserve the evidence (see `EVIDENCE_BASED_OPERATIONS.md`).
4. Determine which tenants, trades, employees, equipment, or projects are affected.
5. Recommend action.
6. Request approval where needed (see `SECURITY.md`'s autonomy tiers).
7. Update operational guidance after approval.
8. Schedule future re-verification.

## Relationship to other documents
